- Object to processing of your Data, if the processing is carried out on a legal basis other than consent.
- Access your Data, to learn if the Data is being processed by us, obtain disclosure regarding certain aspects of the processing, and obtain a copy of the Data undergoing processing.
- Verify and seek rectification, to verify the accuracy of your Data and ask for it to be updated or corrected.
- Restrict the processing of your Data, in which case, we will not process your Data for any purpose other than means required to make the particular Service or Services work.

To exercise these rights, you may submit a request, at any give time, to the contact above in this Policy. Your Data is never sold to or shared with any third party, whether online or offline.
